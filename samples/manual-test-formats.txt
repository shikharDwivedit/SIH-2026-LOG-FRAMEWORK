SIH MANUAL TEST-BENCH CORPUS
=============================

How to use:
1. Open Dashboard -> Test bench.
2. Paste ONE record at a time, without the section heading.
3. Submit it and inspect detected_format, matched_parser, extracted_fields,
   normalized_event, mapped_fields, and vendor_specific_retained.
4. The lines beginning with EXPECTED are notes only; do not paste them.

The --- separators are not part of any log record.
